Architecture & Philosophy

Why Small Language Models?
Precision Over Raw Size.

Massive generalist LLMs are expensive, slow, and prone to hallucinations on specialized domain tasks. Quantune enables any engineer to fine-tune 1B–7B parameter SLMs to outperform 70B giants on narrow corporate and domain workloads.

⚡

Unsloth Response Masking

Training models on the prompt tokens wastes GPU memory and induces catastrophic forgetting. Quantune automatically injects response loss masks, computing gradients strictly on assistant responses to deliver 2x–5x training speedups with 70% VRAM reductions.

🎯

Zero GPU Setup Barrier

No local CUDA debugging, driver headaches, or monthly cloud GPU bills. Quantune compiles your dataset and hyperparameter recommendations directly into an executable, response-masked Google Colab notebook that runs 100% free on a T4 GPU.

🩺

Deep 12-Point Data Diagnostics

90% of fine-tuning failures originate from contaminated, truncated, or unbalanced data. Quantune executes automatic schema normalization, token distribution analysis, and single-click automated repair before a single weight is updated.

🔄

Continuous Refinement Loop

Evaluation shouldn't wait until production deployment. Step 5 provides a side-by-side comparative testing studio where you can contrast raw base SLMs against fine-tuned checkpoints and refine failure cases into new training rows in one click.
